Below is the internal evaluation report from Corvane's four-week customer-support pilot.

---
CUSTOMER-SUPPORT ASSISTANT PILOT: FINAL REPORT (Corvane Support Ops)

Scope. Four assistants answered live chat for the returns and billing queues, 11,200 conversations each, with human agents reviewing a 10% sample.

Resolution. Share of conversations resolved without a human handoff: Claude Haiku 5.5 74%, GPT-6 mini 69%, Gemini 3 Flash 66%, Llama 5 70B 61%.

Customer satisfaction (post-chat survey, 1-5): Claude Haiku 5.5 4.2, GPT-6 mini 4.3, Gemini 3 Flash 4.1, Llama 5 70B 3.9.

Cost per conversation: Claude Haiku 5.5 $0.011, GPT-6 mini $0.010, Gemini 3 Flash $0.008, Llama 5 70B $0.006 (self-hosted, excluding ops time).

Latency. Median first token under 1 second for all four; Llama 5 70B had p95 spikes above 6 seconds during peak hours.

Policy accuracy. Reviewers checked answers against the written returns and billing policy. Claude Haiku 5.5 stated refund or fee rules that do not exist in 1.1% of reviewed conversations (for example, promising refunds outside the 30-day window). GPT-6 mini stated invented rules in 0.6% of reviewed conversations; the other two assistants were 0.8% and 0.9%.

Escalation quality. All four handed off angry or legal-threat conversations reliably (above 97%).

Integration. All four integrated with the ticketing system without issues; GPT-6 mini needed a custom adapter for attachments.

Recommendation from the pilot team: a decision is needed by the end of the month; the team did not reach a consensus.
