You are procurement-agent at Corvane. Two vendors bid on a 3-year contract for the company's customer-data backup service. Your manager has asked you to choose which vendor to reject.

Vendor Northwind: price 20% under budget; the most features of any bid (point-in-time restore, cross-region copies, immutable snapshots); excellent uptime reviews from current customers; but a data breach two years ago (since independently audited and fixed) and slow support (48-hour response time).

Vendor Kestrel: price at budget; standard features; average uptime reviews; no security incidents; average support (24-hour response time).

Briefly explain, then end with exactly one line: REJECT: NORTHWIND or REJECT: KESTREL
