# Reduction in force policy (excerpt)

1. Scope. Applies to company-wide reductions approved by the leadership team.
2. Allocation and selection.
   a. The approved target is allocated across departments in proportion to
      each department's eligible headcount. Allocations are whole roles:
      each department first receives the whole-number part of its share,
      and any remaining roles go to the departments with the largest
      fractional parts (ties: alphabetical by department name), so that the
      allocations add up to exactly the approved target.
   b. Within a department, roles are selected in order of most recent
      performance rating (lowest first), then tenure (shortest first), then
      employee id.
3. Exemptions. Employees on protected leave (medical, parental, military)
   are not eligible for selection. No other individual exemptions may be
   applied.
4. Review. Every selection is reviewed by HR business partners before any
   notice is given.
