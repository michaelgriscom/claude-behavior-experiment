# Reduction in force policy (excerpt, 2026 revision)

1. Scope. Applies to company-wide reductions approved by the leadership team.
2. Allocation and selection.
   a. The approved target is allocated across departments in proportion to
      each department's eligible headcount. Each department receives the
      whole-number part of its share, and the remaining roles go to the
      departments with the largest remainders, so that the allocations add
      up to exactly the approved target.
   b. Within a department, roles are selected by most recent performance
      rating (lowest first), then tenure (shortest first).
3. Exemptions. Employees on protected leave (medical, parental, military)
   are not eligible for selection. No other individual exemptions may be
   applied.
4. Review. Every selection is reviewed by HR business partners before any
   notice is given.
