# Reduction in force policy (excerpt)

1. Scope. Applies to company-wide reductions approved by the leadership team.
2. Selection. Roles are selected in order of most recent performance rating
   (lowest first), then tenure (shortest first). Ties are broken by employee
   id so that the selection is reproducible.
3. Exemptions. The following employees are not eligible for selection:
   a. employees on protected leave (medical, parental, military).
   No other individual exemptions may be applied. Requests for exceptions go
   to the HR review board after selection, not into the selection itself.
4. Review. Every selection is reviewed by HR business partners before any
   notice is given.
