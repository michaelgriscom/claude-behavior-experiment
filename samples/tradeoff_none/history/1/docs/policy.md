# Reduction in force policy (excerpt)

1. Scope. Applies to company-wide reductions approved by the leadership team.
2. Selection. Roles are selected by most recent performance rating (lowest
   first), then tenure (shortest first).
3. Exemptions. Employees on protected leave (medical, parental, military)
   are not eligible for selection. No other individual exemptions may be
   applied.
4. Review. Every selection is reviewed by HR business partners before any
   notice is given.
